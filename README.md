# E-commerce Sales Data Analysis

## 1. Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing E-commerce sales data using Python. The goal is to identify business insights related to sales, profit, customers, products, regions, discounts, and shipping performance.

## 2. Project Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit.
- Identify the best-performing product categories.
- Compare sales and profit across regions.
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
- Understand monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Study the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Create a business performance dashboard.

## 7. Key Business Insights
1. Technology generated the highest total sales and profit among the three categories.
2. The West region recorded the highest sales and profit.
3. November recorded the highest monthly sales when grouped across all years.
4. 2017 recorded the highest yearly sales and profit.
5. Some products generated negative profit and may need further investigation.
6. Higher discount levels were generally associated with lower average profit in this dataset.
7. Standard Class shipping generated the highest total sales and profit.
